### Proposed review mechanism and snapshot rules

- States: **draft**, **technically verified**, **pending human review**, **reviewed revision**, **changed after review**. Automated checks may establish technical verification only. A supplied human event must identify a real decision source, reviewer handle, review date, scope and snapshot; tooling does not manufacture one. Named accountable reviewers remain PENDING. Do not store sensitive/private reviewer data in client-shared records.
- A fingerprint includes the public record, root-layout/transitive public consumer source and local public media bytes. Metadata/schema/remote URL/name/alt/caption and **public publication/material dates** are covered. The TypeScript source printer excludes internal editorial objects, type-only imports, comments and formatting; CSS bytes remain conservatively covered; internal history/review/evidence timestamps do not self-invalidate. A public caption's capture/evidence date remains public content and is covered. Remote bytes are not fetched or certified. Shared consumer sources and the whole nine-file public asset set conservatively invalidate multiple records if changed.
- Old supplied human events are immutable. Full reviews cover every snapshot part, including the public record and individual source/media file identities. Addenda reference the latest event and explicitly cover every changed part, preserving unchanged scope and prior history. Unknown/malformed evidence remains pending. A reviewed editorial revision never makes a PENDING factual claim verified.
- Publication labels currently in the registry/articles and explicit fixed material-update labels are retained as **source-declared history**, not independently certified first-publication evidence. Unknown publication/material dates are omitted. No deployment/request/test/technical-review clock may populate them. A public media addition or substantive text/meta/schema change requires human evaluation of material-update history; no automated date refresh occurs. Internal formatting/governance changes do not refresh public dates.
- Visible/OG/schema publication uses `publishedAt`; visible/OG/schema/MD/MCP modification uses `updatedAt` only. Sitemap `lastmod` may use a supported initial publication when no material update exists, without calling it an update. MD/MCP expose publication separately. Date-only source labels use UTC-midnight normalization for machine/RSS outputs; that normalization does not prove the original publication hour. RSS permits an undated item, so unknown dates are omitted; a future format requiring publication dates must exclude undated items until resolved. Feed channel dates derive from declared item history, not request time.
- Legal effective date/version labels and author joining labels remain their existing meanings; they are never approval, publication or page-update dates. Unanchored careers relative posting labels are omitted; actual recruiting/publication dates and factual job promises remain B19/OI02/OI12. Existing fixed static MCP/solution date labels are retained pending historical evidence; no cross-format date invented to fill unknowns. Public review badges and visitor-facing governance boilerplate were not added.
