## Policy and calculation scope

The October schedule starts at 00:00 in the Messaging account timezone. A customer message opens/resets the 24-hour service window; incoming messages are not charged. From October, delivered Service replies are charged after 1,000 free delivered Service messages **per business phone number per month**, shared across 1:1/group deliveries and Messaging accounts; each delivered group recipient uses one unit. No rollover. Delivered Utility templates are charged within the 24-hour service window unless an eligible free-entry window applies. A qualifying mobile Click-to-WhatsApp ad entry followed by a business reply in that service window can open a free-entry window up to seven days; verified deliveries in that window are excluded, not every ad click. Monthly paid Utility and Authentication tiers accrue separately by market/category across the business portfolio; Authentication international shares Authentication accrual. Its rate additionally requires a listed recipient market and Meta-confirmed eligibility, different primary business location, notice/start-date conditions. `N/A` remains unavailable, never free.

The calculators start with an explicit October India example: 1,000 Marketing and 1,000 Utility deliveries give **₹978.10** Meta delivery subtotal, with no previous paid tier counts or free-entry exclusions. Users enter the actual delivery month, original INR recipient market, counts, confirmed free-entry cohorts, remaining Service allowance for one phone, prior paid tier counts and Authentication treatment. Invalid inputs, unsupported international eligibility or pre-October dates fail closed. Input counts are delivered recipients, not send attempts. Marginal arithmetic uses integer ten-thousandths of INR and rounds the final Meta subtotal once; displayed category amounts may differ from their sum by a paisa. The calculator cannot determine actual Meta eligibility, remaining allowance, free-entry expiry, billing account/currency, or invoiced totals. Users must reconcile account webhooks/invoice. It does not model mixed authentication delivery order automatically; calculate ordered cohorts with the shared prior count. Meta Business Agent/token, Marketing Messages API token/max-price and Calling API charges are outside this delivery estimator.

Whats91 passes Meta messaging charges through without markup. Platform subscription, setup, integrations, taxes and total payable remain separate; existing `src/lib/plans.ts` amounts, entitlements and 18% tax assumption were not altered or newly approved as public payable quotes. Partner/Coins conversion remains held. No payment/provider/private-account/form workflow was exercised.
